About

I am a Staff Scientist in the Department of Human Genetics at Sidra Medicine, working on deep learning for medical imaging and genomics, generative AI, and translational research.

Previously, I was at the Department of Radiology & Biomedical Imaging, Yale University (2024–2026), working on dynamic PET image denoising, generative AI and PET/MR imaging; a Research Fellow at Massachusetts General Hospital and Harvard Medical School (2021–2024); and a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore (2019–2021). I also founded AIPoiesis, an AI startup building explainable AI systems for eye disease classification. I received my Ph.D. from Yonsei University in 2019.
